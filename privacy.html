Privacy Policy - AI Training Coach

Last updated: 2026-11-22

AI Training Coach ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial app built by an individual developer. It is used only by the developer to analyse their own training data. This policy explains which data the app reads, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Data we read

With your authorization through HUAWEI Health Kit, the app reads (read-only) the following data:

  • Activity records (workouts), including sport type, duration, distance, pace, heart rate, cadence and location (GPS track)
  • Steps, distance, ascent and altitude, calories, medium- and high-intensity activity, and daily activity summary
  • Sport ability indicators (for example VO2max)
  • Heart rate and heart health data (for example heart rate variability)
  • Personal information: age, gender, height and weight
  • Notification data: workout-related alarms and reminders
  • Up to one year of historical data for the types above

The app never writes, changes or deletes data in HUAWEI Health.

2. How we use the data

  • To analyse each workout and compare it with previous workouts
  • To calculate training load, heart rate zones and recovery indicators
  • To generate personalised training recommendations

The data is not used for advertising, profiling for third parties, or any medical purpose.

3. Sharing with third parties

To generate the written analysis and training recommendations, workout and health data is sent to the Claude API, an AI service provided by Anthropic PBC. Anthropic processes this data only to return the analysis and, under its commercial terms, does not use API data to train its models. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

No other third party receives the data. The data is never sold.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored privately on systems controlled by the developer, protected by access control. Access tokens for HUAWEI Health Kit are stored securely and never shared. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the app is in use. You can revoke the app's access at any time in the HUAWEI ID or HUAWEI Health privacy settings. After access is revoked, or on request, all stored data is deleted within 30 days.

6. Children

The app is not intended for children and does not knowingly process children's data.
